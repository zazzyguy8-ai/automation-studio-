Frequently asked questions

Do you offer treatment under general anaesthetic? Yes, for anxious patients and longer procedures, by arrangement.

What should I do in a dental emergency outside opening hours? Call the Vienna dental emergency service on 01 512 20 78.

How do I cancel or move an appointment? Use the link in your confirmation email, or call us during opening hours.

Do you treat children? Yes, we have a dedicated paediatric dentist.

Which insurers do you work with? We treat both public and private insurance patients. Please bring your card.

How long does a check-up take? Usually 30 minutes including cleaning.
